Open-source GIF encoder · runs in your browser

Make and compress GIFs in your browser

Turn videos into GIFs, and compress, resize, crop and edit the GIFs you have. Nothing is uploaded: the lessgif encoder runs on your device as WebAssembly.

Command line

$ lessgif clip.mp4 clip.gif --quality 70
$ lessgif big.gif small.gif --max-size 8M

The same encoder is an app for Windows, macOS and Linux, with no limits on file size or length. Download it, or read the code and the benchmark on GitHub.
