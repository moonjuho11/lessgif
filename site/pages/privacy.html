Privacy

The short version: the files you open on this site are never uploaded. They are read and processed by code running in your own browser tab.

Your files

Videos, GIFs and images you open are processed on your device with JavaScript and WebAssembly. They are not sent to this website's server or to anyone else. When you pass a result from one tool to another, it is kept in your browser's own storage on this device until the next result replaces it.

What the server sees

Like any website, the hosting provider that serves these pages receives the usual technical details of each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page asked for, and may keep them in short-lived logs for security and reliability.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Advertising

This site shows ads from Google AdSense to pay for itself. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, which may be personalised based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. The ads never see the files you open.
