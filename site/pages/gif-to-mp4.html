GIF to MP4

Turn a GIF into an MP4 video, for sites and apps that don't take GIFs. The video is usually much smaller than the GIF.

About GIF to MP4

The video uses H.264, the format every phone, browser and social app plays. Each frame keeps its own timing, so the video plays at the same speed as the GIF. A video stores colours far more efficiently than a GIF, so it usually comes out several times smaller, though very sharp pixel art can look a little softer.

Which browsers can do this?

Making the video uses your browser's built-in video encoder: Chrome, Edge and Safari have one for H.264, and so does Firefox on most systems. Where a browser can't make H.264, it makes the MP4 with VP9 instead, which plays in browsers and on most phones but may not be accepted by every app.
