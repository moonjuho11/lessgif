Make a GIF from images

Put pictures together into an animated GIF: photos for a slideshow, or frames for an animation. Add GIFs too and all their frames come along.

Tips

Frames are used in the order shown; use the arrows to move them, or sort them by file name. Each frame has its own delay in milliseconds, and Set all gives every frame the same one. Pictures of different shapes are fitted into the size of the first one, with the rest of the frame left transparent.

Is there a limit?

Only your device's memory. A few hundred frames at a normal size work fine in most browsers.
